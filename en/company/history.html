History

History
  • 1918
    November
    Asahi Warehouse Co., Ltd. established with capital of 1 million yen, at the initiative of influential figures in the Japanese pharmaceutical and dye trade.
  • 1928
    May
    In Odai-cho, Adachi Ward, in addition to regular warehouses, the only large-scale hazardous material storage warehouse in Tokyo was constructed. The Adachi Sales Office, focusing primarily on the storage of chemicals, was opened.
  • 1951
    December
    The capital was increased to 4 million yen.
  • 1958
    October
    The capital was increased to 16 million yen.
  • 1962
    April
    The capital was increased to 32 million yen.
  • 1966
    February
    A transportation department was newly established, and the company obtained a license for motor vehicle transportation business. A comprehensive logistics system was established.
  • 1966
    March
    The Matsudo Sales Office was opened in Matsudo-shi, Chiba.
  • 1968
    December
    Asahi Unyu Co., Ltd. was established to expand the transportation business.
  • 1970
    September
    The Kashiwa Office was opened in Nagareyama-shi, Chiba.
  • 1973
    August
    The Itabashi Sales Office was opened as a tenant of Tokyo Industrial Warehouse Co., Ltd.'s Itabashi Warehouse.
  • 1976
    November
    The Adachi Distribution Center was opened as a tenant of Tokyo Industrial Warehouse Co., Ltd.'s Adachi Warehouse.
  • 1977
    December
    Asahi Distribution Service Co., Ltd. was established to efficiently manage warehouse handling operations.
  • 1982
    February
    The capital was increased to 64 million yen.
  • 1982
    August
    The Kawasaki Sales Office was opened at the Toashishima Foreign Trade Wharf in Kawasaki-shi.
  • 1984
    April
    Started a real estate leasing business for the redevelopment of the Tsukishima Office and business expansion.
  • 1989
    May
    Asahi Logistics Consultant Co., Ltd. was established to strengthen the logistics information network system.
  • 1991
    April
    Started a self-storage business targeting general consumers.
  • 1993
    June
    The second phase of construction at the Kawasaki Sales Office was completed.
  • 2000
    October
    Closed the Adachi Sales Office due to the redevelopment of the Adachi Odai area.
  • 2000
    November
    Dissolved Asahi Distribution Service Co., Ltd. after completing its role.
  • 2001
    February
    To strengthen information system management, Asahi Logistics Consultant Co., Ltd. was dissolved, and the function was centralized within the company.
  • 2004
    May
    The Asahi Rainbow Square was completed in Adachi Odai.
  • 2006
    May
    Received Privacy Mark certification (JIS Q 15001).
  • 2007
    May
    Obtained permission for general worker dispatching business.
  • 2007
    November
    Received Quality Management System certification (ISO 9001, JIS Q 9001).
  • 2008
    March
    The Nippori-Toneri Liner opened in Adachi Odai.
  • 2011
    April
    The Adachi Distribution Center was renamed and reopened as the Adachi Sales Office.
  • 2018
    November
    Asahi Warehouse Co., Ltd. celebrated its 100th anniversary.
  • 2024
    June
    Obtained AEO (Authorized Economic Operator) certified bonded warehouse authorization.
