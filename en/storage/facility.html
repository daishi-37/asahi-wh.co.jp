Storage Facility

  • The location is highly accessible.
    Situated inland with no concerns of flooding, the facility is in a prime location within Tokyo's 23 wards, providing outstanding access. The storage floor (document shelves) is more than 12 meters above ground level.
    According to the 'Seismic Hazard Risk Assessment Survey' published by the Tokyo Metropolitan Bureau of Urban Development, the area where the document storage facility is located has the lowest risk in all categories, including building collapse risk, fire risk, and overall risk.
  • Stored in a dedicated document storage facility. 
    We manage each box individually in a dedicated document storage warehouse. Since we do not use pallet storage or stacked storage, we ensure secure storage.
    • Self-storage
      Pricing is determined by the dimensions of the space.
    • Document storage facility
      Starting with just one box, it is available at an affordable price, making management simple
  • Each box is managed by data. 
    For each box, we will attach management labels (business card size) for data management.
    Additionally, we have prepared dedicated storage boxes made of one-touch cardboard.
    Simply spreading it out allows for quick bottom assembly (no tape required).
  • We protect information thorough building access control management.
    The entrance to the document storage floor is equipped with electronic locks, and only authorized personnel are allowed to enter and leave the building with their security cards, thus ensuring thorough building access control management. 
    We have acquired the Privacy Mark and ISO9001 certifications, and promise high-quality, reliable services.
