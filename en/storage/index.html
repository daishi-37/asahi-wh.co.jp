Regarding Document Storage
Service Fees
The fees may vary depending on the quantity of stored items,
future increases or decreases in the volume of goods,
the frequency of storage retrievals, and other factors.
For more details, please contact us.
Inquiries
  • The storage fee is charged on a per-box basis, calculated monthly for each document box stored.
  • Additionally, based on your request, other fees such as transportation, retrieval and storage handling, viewing, disposal, and document storage box sales and shipping may be charged.
