  • Easy-to-understand unit price setting

    • Staple removal & restoration (1 location): from 25 yen 
    • Because the pricing can vary based on the condition and filing method of the documents, we will give a price estimate only after reviewing the actual documents.
  • Supports large-scale drawings and related formats.
    Compatible sizes 
    • B3/A2, B2/A1, B1/A0 sizes 
    • We also accommodate large-format sizes of A2 and above. Additionally, we can handle the disassembly and re-binding scanning of various completed documents (additional fees apply for scanning services).
  • We will remove staples and clips for scanning, and we also accommodate sticky notes.
    The standard procedure is to scan without removing sticky notes.
    If there is printing on the back of the sticky note:
    The sticky note will be removed for scanning, and it will be reattached to its original position after scanning.
    If the sticky note is used as a memo pad:
    The sticky note will be reattached to a space without printing on the back for scanning, and it will be placed back in its original position after scanning. The sticky note will be removed for scanning, and it will be reattached to its original position after scanning.

    Basic Fees:
    • PDF Name Assignment: 20 yen per PDF
    • Folder Name Assignment: 45 yen per folder
    • We need a list or clear guidelines for assignment.
