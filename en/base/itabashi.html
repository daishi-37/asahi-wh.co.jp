Features of the Itabashi Sales Office

  • 01
    Easy access
    Situated inland, where there are no concerns about flooding, this location offers excellent access within the 23 wards of Tokyo. The storage floor (document storage shelves) is positioned over 12 meters above ground.
  • 02
    Carefully stored in a dedicated document storage facility
    We manage each box individually in a dedicated document storage warehouse. Since we do not use pallet storage or stacked storage, we can ensure secure storage.
    • Self-Storage
      Fees are determined by the size of the space.
    • Document Storage Facility
      Available at an affordable rate starting from just one box, with easy management.
  • 03
    Equipped with a reading room
    Since there will be a shipping fee for the delivery of document boxes, if you only need a few pages from the contents, you can use the reading room instead.
    • Convenient
      You can also make copies of the documents.
    • Cost reduction
      This helps save on transportation expenses.
  • 04
    Thorough building access control management
    An electronic lock has been installed at the entrance of the document storage floor, and access will be granted only to authorized personnel who possess a security card.
  • 05
    We have obtained various certifications and accreditations
    We provide the quality and security required for storing confidential documents at a service level that has been accredited by a third party.
    Since we handle personal and confidential information in our operations, we implement strict management based on rules and security facilities compliant with the Privacy Mark.
