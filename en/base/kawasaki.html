Features of the Kawasaki Sales Office

  • 01
    Strict pest control management system
    In addition to pest control management by external contractors, we have initiated an internal pest control project to prevent contamination by foreign objects.
    We prioritize a dust and debris free warehouse, managing our customers' valuable products in a sanitary environment using dust-proof paint.
  • 02
    Food safety manual
    We operate our own unique Food Safety Manual, which includes restrictions on the management areas for remarketed products, storage restrictions for allergens, records of chemical usage, and cleaning records.

    ●Full Implementation of 5S
    Besides keeping products organized and well maintained, we make every effort to provide a hygienic environment.
  • 03
    AEO Specific Customs Approval System / ISO 9001 Certification Acquisition
    With the approval as an AEO specific customs authorized operator, we are establishing a compliance framework that contributes to strengthening and improving the efficiency of our risk management capabilities.
    Additionally, following the acquisition of ISO 9001 certification, we are actively working to improve the warehouse environment and establish high-quality logistics management.
  • 04
    Food Distribution and Processing Center
    As a processing facility for food distribution, including wine, food, and beverages, we offer comprehensive services such as foreign object inspection, exterior inspection, shipment packing, and wrapping.
  • 05
    High-quality temperature control
    Grains, wine, champagne, and other products require temperature control tailored to their specific characteristics.
    To carefully store our customers' delicate products, we offer a temperature-controlled warehouse capable of maintaining a temperature of 17°C ± 2°C, equipped with facilities that allow for strict temperature management.
    We also provide various data necessary for storage management, including warehouse temperature records and air conditioning maintenance records.
  • 06
    Various services for imported cargo
    We have a customs-approved bonded warehouse from Yokohama Customs and are fully prepared to handle import and export cargo. Additionally, we are capable of various cargo handling operations, including customs procedures, various container transport, DEVAN operations, and VANNING operations.
  • 07
    Web inquiry System
    With our inventory management web system, you can check inventory status in real-time on your PC (compatible with smartphones).
