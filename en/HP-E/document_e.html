Document Storage


We keep the important documents of your company with the newest system by a thoroughgoing security system and thoroughgoing air-conditioning, and computer.
Only these documents are kept and managed in the storage warehouse which is equipped with the air conditioner in the building of the earthquake-proof and fire-resistant structure excellent in safety.










Boxes for only documents storage are no charge (a transportation charge 1,050 JPY, inclusive of tax, per ten sheets is required).A document may be perused in a free private room.

Moreover, when the document reaches its expiry date, a certificate of dissolution will be sent to customers as confirmation of the dissolution process.


It is also possible to make the contents of the custody document into a PDF file, and to send it by E-mail (please consult).
