ISO Certification

Our company is certified under the ISO 9001 Quality Management System standard.
ISO (International Organization for Standardization) is a non‑governmental organization established in 1947 to develop international standards for industrial and commercial fields. National standardization bodies from around the world participate as members.

Its main role is to create and promote international standards that improve the quality, safety, and efficiency of products and services. These standards help ensure compatibility across countries and regions, facilitating smoother international trade.

Examples include ISO 9001 for quality management, ISO 14001 for environmental management, and ISO 27001 for information security management. These standards serve as guidelines for organizations to enhance operational efficiency and improve customer satisfaction.

AEO Program

In 2024, we were approved as an 'Authorized Warehouse Operator' under the AEO program.
The Authorized Economic Operator (AEO) program is a framework established to enhance the security and efficiency of international trade. Under this system, companies recognized for their strong security management and compliance practices are granted benefits such as simplified and expedited customs procedures.

In practice, AEO-certified companies enjoy streamlined import and export processes, significantly reducing customs clearance times. This improvement enhances logistics efficiency and contributes to substantial cost savings. In addition, because certified companies meet rigorous safety and risk‑management standards, they earn a high level of trust from business partners and customs authorities alike.

The AEO program has also gained global traction, with many countries mutually recognizing each other’s certifications through Mutual Recognition Agreements (MRAs). As a result, certified companies can access similar benefits in overseas transactions, enabling smoother global business operations.

Overall, the AEO program plays a crucial role in promoting secure and efficient international trade, strengthening corporate competitiveness. By obtaining AEO certification, companies can conduct trade activities with greater safety and efficiency, accelerating their business growth.

PrivacyMark System

We obtained the PrivacyMark certification in 2006 and manage personal information in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
The PrivacyMark System is a Japanese certification program designed to ensure proper protection of personal information. Operated by the Japan Information Processing Development Corporation (JIPDEC), it evaluates whether companies and organizations appropriately manage personal information. Those that meet the required standards are granted the “PrivacyMark.”

The assessment examines whether an organization has established and properly operates a management system for protecting personal information. Obtaining the PrivacyMark demonstrates a high level of reliability in personal information protection.
